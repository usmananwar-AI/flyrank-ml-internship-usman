2. Real vs. AI Decision Rationale
Real captures were chosen strictly for all evidence of machine learning work (Data Contracts, Feature Engineering). AI-generated code, faux dashboards, or abstract math equations look immediately fake to technical recruiters and undermine your credibility. A real photograph is used for the profile to build human trust. AI generation is explicitly restricted to the hero texture, where its purpose is solely to establish a quiet visual mood.

3. The Rejection Note (Judgment Exercise)
I rejected an AI-generated image of a glowing blue, wireframe brain connected to a futuristic server rack. While it successfully incorporated the sky blue accent color, the concept was heavily cliché and overly busy. It competed for attention rather than acting as a subtle canvas, failing the core rule that the design must frame the work, not upstage it.
